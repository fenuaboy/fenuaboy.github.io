    Filozen Daily XL: a new number path puzzle every day
    
  
    Connect the numbers in order with one single thread that passes through every cell. Today’s grid is the same for everyone, so share your time and see who clears the fog fastest. Free, no ads, no account.
    
  Get the free iPhone app · 50 levels

  
    How to play
    
  
    Start on 1. Draw one continuous thread, cell by cell, without lifting your finger. Reach 2, then 3, and so on, in order. The thread can never cross itself or a wall, and it must pass through every cell of the grid exactly once. When the last cell is filled on the last number, you win.
    
  
    On a computer, use the arrow keys (or WASD, or ZQSD on a French keyboard): the thread moves one cell at a time, and pressing back toward the previous cell undoes the last step. Backspace undoes too. On a phone, just drag your finger.
    
  
    Stuck? Go back along your thread to undo, or drag from any point to change direction. The timer only counts while you play.
    

  
    Playing LinkedIn Zip every morning?
    
  
    Then you already know the idea. Filozen gives you a bigger challenge here: an 8×8 grid every day with walls to work around. On iPhone, the app adds its own daily grid, 50 handcrafted levels and unlimited free play.
    

  
    Share your result
    
  
    When you finish, tap Share. You get a short message with your time and a clarity score, ready to send on WhatsApp, Messages or social media, with no spoilers of the solution.
    

  
    Questions
    
  
    Is it the same grid for everyone?
    Yes. Everyone gets the same XL grid each day. A new one arrives at midnight, your local time.
    
  
    Is Filozen free?
    Yes. The daily grid is free in your browser, and the iPhone app is free with no ads.
    
  
    Does it work on a computer?
    Yes. Use the arrow keys or the mouse on a computer, or your finger on a phone or tablet.
